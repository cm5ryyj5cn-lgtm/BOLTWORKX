# BoltWorkx

## Simple job management for modern tradespeople

BoltWorkx is a focused job-management workspace for solo tradespeople and small teams. It brings customers, estimates, invoices, scheduling, expenses, staff and daily planning into one clean cyber-style dashboard.

It is designed for busy businesses such as window cleaners, electricians, plumbers, handymen, decorators, landscapers and other mobile service professionals who want less paperwork and better control of their work.

## What BoltWorkx helps with

BoltWorkx gives a small trade business one place to:

- Keep customer contact and site details
- Track stock quantities and low-stock levels for products and materials
- Record completed sales, reduce tracked stock, and review takings that reset daily
- View monthly sales takings with a configurable reset day (defaulting to the 1st) in Settings
- Create jobs and estimates with labour, products and materials
- Track job status from draft through to completion
- Mark invoices as Paid or Outstanding
- Create printable invoices
- Schedule future work on a calendar
- Create weekly, fortnightly or monthly recurring jobs
- Repeat a previous job quickly
- Plan routes with customer addresses and map links
- Set customer reminder dates
- Record business, fuel and mileage expenses
- Add receipt photos to expenses
- Review monthly income, expenses, tax estimates and net income
- Print a paper-style financial report
- Manage staff wages, roles, contact details and annual leave
- Create a colour-coded staff rota calendar
- Export customers, jobs, sales, staff and stock records as CSV files
- Back up and restore the complete workspace
- In supported browsers, choose a backup file once and automatic backups overwrite that same file while BoltWorkx is open

## Built for small teams

The Staff area supports hourly wages and annual salaries, annual leave allowances, leave taken and leave remaining. Each staff member can have a custom rota colour, making it easier to see who is working on each day.

## Private-by-default storage

BoltWorkx currently runs as a standalone HTML application. Records are stored in the browser's local storage on the device being used. There is no account system, automatic cloud synchronisation or external database in this version.

Users should regularly use the backup feature and keep exported copies in a secure location. Do not store sensitive customer information on a shared or public computer.

## How to use it

1. Open `BoltWorkx.html` in a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge or Safari.
2. Open Settings and enter the business name, VAT rate, tax estimate and colour theme.
3. Add customers and products or services.
4. Create jobs, estimates and invoices.
5. Use Schedule, Income and Staff to manage the wider business workflow.
6. Save a backup in Settings to choose the file used for automatic overwrites, then select an interval. Keep BoltWorkx open for scheduled backups to run.

Browsers without file-access support download separate backup files instead; they cannot silently overwrite a previous download.

## Product positioning

BoltWorkx is intended to be simpler and more approachable than large field-service platforms. It focuses on the practical daily needs of small trade businesses without requiring a complex setup.

The current standalone version is best suited to individual businesses using one browser profile. A future hosted version could add secure accounts, cloud synchronisation, shared team access, online payments, automatic reminders and accounting integrations.

## Important note

Tax figures are planning estimates only and are not tax advice. Users remain responsible for checking wages, annual leave, tax, invoices, privacy obligations and other legal requirements that apply to their business.
